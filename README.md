4D Betting System

A complete online 4D betting platform with a website, a members’ area, an agent network and an Android app. Results are collected automatically, bets are settled automatically, and built-in risk controls protect the house.

Betting
13 companies: Magnum, Da Ma Cai, Sports Toto, Singapore Pools, Sabah 88, Sandakan, Sarawak Cash Sweep, GD Lotto, Perdana, Lucky HariHari, Nine Lotto, plus the Perdana and HariHari 3:30pm draws. The admin chooses which companies are on sale.
Bet types:
4D: Big, Small, 4A, 4SB, 4SC, 4SD, 4SE and 4C.
3D: ABC and A.
Toto 5D and 6D.
Normal, Box and iBox play, priced automatically.
Two ways to bet:
a simple form for a few numbers;
a fast spreadsheet-style grid for many numbers, with fill-down and tick-all shortcuts.
Several draw dates and companies on one slip. Days when a company doesn’t draw are left out automatically.
Bet slips come with a QR code, print on paper or Bluetooth thermal printers, have a “Bet again” button, and can be marked as paid out.
Results
Automatic collection from each operator’s official website, cross-checked against independent result sites.
Results confirmed by two sources are approved automatically. When sources disagree, the admin chooses which to use.
Result corrections re-settle only the bets that change: unpaid winnings are cancelled and paid winnings are taken back.
Public results page:
all companies’ results, plus display-only jackpots, lotto games and 3+3D;
a “check your number” tool that can also search permutations;
past results by date.
Settlement and winnings
Bets settle automatically once results are verified, using the prize rates the admin sets.
Winnings wait for admin approval before they are paid into the member’s wallet. Approval can be done one at a time or in bulk.
Bets that can’t be decided automatically (for example, a missing prize rate) are held for review.
Draw cancellation refunds every stake on that draw automatically.
Risk control
Payout pool:
a maximum payout per number per draw;
a worst-case limit per draw day across all companies.
Maximum stake per customer per number, set separately for each bet type.
Red numbers: numbers blocked permanently or for one draw, optionally with every permutation. Numbers that reach the pool limit are blocked automatically.
Live dashboard showing exposure, numbers close to their limit and the profit or loss of each draw.
Agent network
Three levels: agents, dealers and end users.
Agent terms: commission (a share of sales, no risk) or risk share (a share of sales and of wins). Defaults apply to every agent, and each agent can have their own terms.
Agents give credit to dealers, approve top-ups, and see their earnings and statements.
End users can sign up themselves (the admin can switch this on or off). They top up by bank transfer and upload the receipt for checking.
Promotions
House jackpot (admin can switch it on or off):
Jackpot 1 “Double Strike”;
Jackpot 2 “1st Prize Hit”;
funded by a percentage of stakes, with a starting amount and a cap, and every win approved by the admin;
shown only to members who are signed in.
Rebate plans: end users get back a percentage of their net loss for each draw day, credited to their wallet automatically.
Administration
A dashboard covering today’s bets, results to check, winnings to approve, member wallets and pool usage.
A full wallet history for every member. Corrections are made as new entries; past entries are never edited.
Excel export of bets and winnings, filtered as needed.
Prize rates, draw calendar, betting close times, company bank account and site settings.
Android app
Everything a member can do on the website: betting (simple form and grid), slips, results, winnings, wallet and top-up.
Bluetooth thermal printing of slips, and QR scanning to open a printed slip or bet again.
Jackpot and rebate cards on the home screen.
International
One base currency per site, chosen in settings: MYR, SGD, USD, AUD or AED.
Three languages: English, 中文 and Bahasa Melayu, on the website, the admin pages and the app. Each user picks their own.
Security and reliability
HTTPS only and protection against forged requests.
Limits on repeated sign-in attempts, and separate access for admins, agents, dealers and end users.
Every money movement is recorded and checked against the wallet balance.
Before release, the system passed 330 automated simulation checks covering settlement, payouts, refunds, corrections, agent earnings, jackpots and rebates. It was also stress-tested with more than 30,000 bet records in one day.
